How to use your jacket

Can one line of clothing create more meaningful interactions?

  1. 1. Say hello and ask

    She catches his eye outside the café. “Hey, I like your jacket. What’s the shield on the back? Can I scan it?”

  2. 2. Turn and scan

    “Sure,” he says, turning so she can see the shield. She opens her camera, points it at the QR code and taps the link that appears.

  3. 3. Keep talking

    The QR code gives them an excuse to chat. She puts her phone down, they share a laugh, and two strangers might just become friends.

The QR code shown is a mockup. Where it leads can vary.
